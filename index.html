PROMISES

Promises the rules keep.

Each promise is a line of code in your own Allot Vault on Robinhood Chain. When a rule says no, the contract reverts with the reason below and nothing moves.

⌁01A cap per payment

Your AI can't pay more than the limit you set for a single request, whatever it's told.

revert "over the per payment cap"
◷02An x402 limit per day

When today's x402 limit is used up, payments stop until tomorrow.

revert "over today's x402 limit"
◎03Only payees you approve

A new address is refused until you add it yourself.

revert "payee not on the list"
⬡04A contract, not a promise

A leaked AI key still can't spend more: the rules sit in the vault, not in the AI or on a server.

AllotFactory 0xc262…9092 ↗
⏻05Revoke or freeze in one click

Switch an AI off, or freeze the whole vault. The next payment fails on chain.

revert "agent not active" · "vault frozen"
≡06No unlimited approvals

Each payment is a one-time EIP-3009 authorization for its exact amount, used once.

authorize(to, exact amount, nonce)
