PROMISES

Promises the rules keep.

Each promise is a line of code in your own Allot Vault on Robinhood Chain. When a rule says no, the contract reverts with the reason below and nothing moves.

⌁01A cap per payment

Your AI can't pay more than the limit you set for a single request, whatever it's told.

revert "over the per payment cap"
◷02An x402 limit per day

When today's x402 limit is used up, payments stop until tomorrow.

revert "over today's x402 limit"
◎03Only payees you approve

A new address is refused until you add it yourself.

revert "payee not on the list"
⬡04A contract, not a promise

A leaked AI key still can't spend more: the rules sit in the vault, not in the AI or on a server.

BUILD
Sell your API to AIs.

    
Charge AI agents per call in USDG on Robinhood Chain, with x402. Start from the Allot sample API: one Cloudflare Worker, no wallet key and no funds on your server. Payments are checked from the receipt on chain before you answer.

    

      
no key on the server
The payer settles its own EIP-3009 authorization. Your worker only reads the receipt from the chain.

      
paid before you answer
No payment, no data: the API answers 402 with an exact price and a one-time nonce bound to the request.

      
AIs with a vault can pay
Any AI with an Allot vault pays inside its owner's rules: cap, daily x402 limit and approved payees.

    

    

      
01
Copy the template
Open github.com/byte402/allot-api ↗ and copy worker.js. In Cloudflare, create a Worker, open Edit code, paste it and deploy.

      
02
Set two settings
In the Worker's Settings, Variables and Secrets: PAY_TO is the address that receives USDG, and CHALLENGE_SECRET is a secret of 32 or more random characters that signs each nonce. Until both are set, paid routes answer 503.

      
03
Add your endpoint
One entry in ROUTES: a price, a canonical query and the data to return. This example sells the live gas price for 0.001 USDG. Put your own data in run.
worker.js

  // price is in USDG base units (6 decimals): 1000n = 0.001 USDG

    gasPriceGwei: Number(BigInt(await rpc("eth_gasPrice", []))) / 1e9,
    at: new Date().toISOString(),
  }) }) },

How your worker checks a payment
    
      
  1. Without payment it answers 402 with the price, your payee and a nonce: an HMAC of the request and an expiry, so nothing is stored.
    2. 
      
  2. The payer settles its own EIP-3009 authorization in USDG and sends the transaction hash in the payment-signature header.
    3. 
      
  3. Your worker reads the receipt from Robinhood Chain: the transaction succeeded, at least the price reached PAY_TO, and AuthorizationUsed carries the nonce it issued.
    4. 
      
  4. One transaction buys one answer. A replayed transaction is refused by the data center that served it; for strict one-time use across all of them, see Security.
    5.

SECURITY
What holds, and what doesn't yet.

    
Every rule your AI follows is enforced by the vault contract on Robinhood Chain, not by the AI or by us. Here is what that code guarantees, its known limits, and how to report anything you find. The contracts are verified on Sourcify and not audited yet.


    
GUARANTEED BY THE CONTRACT
Read it in AllotVault.sol

    

      
Only your wallet controls the vault
Adding an AI, its payees, freeze, unfreeze and withdraw are onlyOwner. There is no admin key and no upgrade path. The factory only deploys and lists vaults, and keeps no control over them.

      
Every AI payment passes every rule
Before any transfer, _spend refuses if the vault is frozen, the AI is not active, its key has expired, the payee is not on its list, the amount is over the cap, or today's x402 limit would be passed.

      
Exact, one-time authorizations
For x402 the vault approves one EIP-3009 transfer by its exact digest. USDG asks isValidSignature, and the answer is yes only for that digest, and never while the vault is frozen. No open allowance is left behind.

      
Unsettled payments give the limit back
An authorization that was never settled can be cancelled with release by the AI or the owner. On the same UTC day its amount returns to the AI's limit.

    


    
KNOWN LIMITS
Stated plainly

    

      
NOT AUDITED
No audit yet
The Factory and Vault are verified on Sourcify with an exact match, but no audit firm has reviewed them. Keep balances small.

      
REVOKE
Revoke stops new payments
revoke turns an AI off at once, but an authorization it made just before stays valid until it expires, about 5 minutes with the connector. To stop it now, freeze the vault or call release. That amount was already inside the AI's rules.

      
TRUST
What you rely on
The USDG token contract and its EIP-3009 and ERC-1271 handling, Robinhood Chain itself, and the machine your AI key lives on. A leaked AI key can still only pay approved payees, within the cap and the daily limit.

      
SAMPLE API
Replay check is per data center
The sample API remembers settled payments in Cloudflare's cache, which is kept per data center. The same payment replayed elsewhere could get a second answer: a cost to the API owner, never to the buyer. For strict one-time use, store them in Workers KV or a Durable Object.
